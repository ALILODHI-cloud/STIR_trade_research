### Three bugs the tests caught, all silent

1. `quarterly_symbols` used a day-of-month heuristic and kept the Sep-26
   contract at the strip front on 19 Sep, after its 16 Sep IMM expiry. Now
   uses the real IMM date.
2. `history()` built a DataFrame from a RangeIndex Series while passing a
   DatetimeIndex. Pandas ALIGNED them, NaN-ing every price, and dropna()
   then returned an empty frame with no error. `.to_numpy()` is load-bearing.
3. `probe_roots` retried all four candidates on a missing key or a blocked
   host, repeating one message four times and burying the real cause. Both
   are environment-level and now abort immediately.
