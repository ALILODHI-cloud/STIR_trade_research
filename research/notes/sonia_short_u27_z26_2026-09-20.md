## 2. Mechanism vs the data

Since August, **β(ΔU27 \| ΔZ26) ≈ 2.0** (YTD ~1.0). On Z26 selloff days
(&gt;+0.5 bp), mean Δ(U27−Z26) = **+1.13 bp** — spread *steepens*, not flattens.

So in the live hike-repricing regime, Dec’26 does **not** drag the peak flat;
the peak **outruns** the front. Shorting U27−Z26 is a bet that this **stops**
(path stops extending / terminal tops / mean-reversion), not a bet that
“front sells → automatic flatten.”
